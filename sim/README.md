# Local Evil Pumpkin simulator

Our 8 leeks (V10) against the Pumpkin army on the local generator: free, about 15 s a fight, 4 in parallel.

```bash
python3 tools/local_pumpkin.py --n 20 --parallel 4 --tag c3          # 20 seeds -> data/sim_pumpkin/c3_<seed>.json
python3 tools/pumpkin_calibrate.py --sim 'data/sim_pumpkin/c3_*.json'  # vs our last 20 real 8-leek fights (v16)
python3 tools/local_pumpkin.py --n 20 --tag arm_b --ai V10/v10_b.py   # an A/B arm: another V10 build
```

## Files

| File | Role |
|---|---|
| `sim/pumpkin_mob.leek` | The mobs' AI (pumpkin, hubbard, turban, warty, offspring), LeekScript |
| `sim/pumpkin_riddle.leek` | Riddle encoding tables, generated by `tools/pumpkin_sim_tables.py` |
| `sim/generator_mob.patch` | Generator patch: scenario entities take `type` (4 = mob), `skin`, `dead`; `mob/Mob.java` |
| `tools/local_pumpkin.py` | Scenario builder + runner: geometry, map and our stats from real fight 54241302, Pumpkin kits from `boss_swap.PK_KITS` / `cure_kit.KITS` |
| `tools/pumpkin_fights.py` | Parser for real and simulated Pumpkin fights (turn records, Glagolitic, riddle, LoS, BFS) |
| `tools/pumpkin_calibrate.py` | Real vs sim: deaths by round, damage per round, drops, per-mob damage / chips / MP |

Setup: the generator needs the patch (`git apply sim/generator_mob.patch` in `leek-wars-generator`, then
`./gradlew jar`) and the symlink `leek-wars-generator/SIM -> LeekWars-AI/sim`.

## What the mobs do

The official mob AI is server-side and unpublished. This one is rebuilt from 708 real Pumpkin fights (other squads' and ours).

**Riddle: exact on the logs.**
- The mobs speak in cycles of K 3-digit Glagolitic numbers. K = min(3, alive mobs) at the cycle's start.
- The mob whose turn comes once K numbers are out holds the slot:
  - a brother is dead and the riddle cell is free: "ⱂⱃⰻⰲⰵⱅ ⰱⱃⰰⱅ!" and awakening on a random dead brother there;
  - a brother is dead and the cell is taken: "ⰽⱁⱂⰻⰾⰵ!!" and exasperation on itself, which drops its Divine Protection;
  - nobody is dead: "ⱆⰿⱃⰻ!".
- The slot rule fits 5,983 of 6,034 slot turns in other squads' 8- and 4-leek fights. All 1,586 of their resurrections land on the riddle cell of the cycle's K numbers.
- The 2nd and 3rd numbers are always said. The first one is said with a probability that depends on alive leeks and mobs (`PSTART`: 1.0 with 8 leeks, about 0.2 with 1).
- Target cells are random free cells: never an obstacle, never occupied when picked. They're no closer to our leeks and no more visible from them than random cells are.

**Combat: fitted.**
- Each mob picks a primary target, then the aim cell that hits it hardest and splashes the most other leeks. Area damage is 1 − 0.2 × distance to the aimed cell.
- Targets follow the real turn 1s, which were identical in all 20 v17 fights:
  - the pumpkin and hubbard take the lowest-life leek they can reach (GTGabi, Kirby);
  - turban and offspring take the nearest;
  - warty poisons clusters (Kirby and ProdigalSon together, not the nearer lone DuskHope).
- Reach includes jumps over obstacles: the pumpkin walks to 357 and jumps to 408 on turn 1, as on the server.
- Steroid with the TP the attacks leave; jump when ready; adrenaline only to afford one more shot.
- Hubbard's inversion on the nearest enemy.
- The pumpkin's scythe: walking, or teleporting onto the weakest leek within 13 when teleport is ready.

## Calibration

**2026-10-23, d2:** 20 sim fights vs the 20 real v17 fights. The template is real fight 54297638, so stats and turn order are current.

| Metric | Real v17 | Sim |
|---|---|---|
| Our dead by round 4 / 8 / 12 | 1.4 / 3.9 / 6.3 | 1.4 / 4.3 / 6.6 |
| Damage taken / fight | 41.5k | 41.5k |
| Damage dealt / fight | 12.5k | 10.7k |
| Kopile drops / fight | 6.7 | 5.0 |
| Resurrections / fight | 4.2 | 4.5 |
| GTGabi's death round (median) | 4 | 4 |
| Fight length | 25 | 17 |

- Turn 1 matches move for move for all four mobs.
- Known gaps:
  - about 1.7 fewer drops a fight and 15% less damage dealt;
  - the late game is shorter, because the last survivors hide better on the server.
- **Re-point `--template` after any respec or component change.** Stats and frequencies set the turn order, and the v16 template (DawnFall frequency 190, today 169) put the mobs on the wrong leeks.
- Older line, c3 vs v16: dealt 10.8k vs 11.2k, taken 43.9k vs 41.7k. The sim then predicted v17's damage at 16.0k; the server gave 12.5k, because our strikers lived too long in the sim.

## Calibration 2026-11-01 (shield-aware mob targets, no stat fudge)

The live pumpkin / hubbard / turban hits match the engine's damage formula exactly (unshielded and per
relative-shield bucket); the old sim was too soft because its mobs ignored shields when choosing targets
(live: 9% of mob hits land on leeks with 75%+ relative shield, shield-blind sim: 31%). pumpkin_mob.leek now
targets by effective life (life / (1 - relative shield) + absolute shield) and weights area aims by the damage
that gets through. Against the 400 live v23 fights (80 sim seeds, same AI, kits and builds):

| | live | sim |
|---|---|---|
| damage dealt | 50.1k | 50.7k |
| damage taken | 40.0k | 38.5k |
| kills | 2.81 | 3.00 |
| dead by round 8 / 12 | 4.62 / 6.64 | 4.29 / 6.55 |
| fight length | 13.6 | 13.8 |
| win rate | 12.8% | 13.8% |
| mob resurrections | 1.0 | 2.2 |

Known gap: late in a fight (3 leeks or fewer alive) the sim's mobs act back to back more often than live, so
more slots have no leek of ours in the window - the sim is pessimistic about blocking.
